A backend project for turning product specifications into marketplace-ready inventory feeds. It parses product data, maps attributes, and generates XSLT files for downstream transformations.

An end-to-end system for monitoring CNC milling machines, validating operator inputs, and predicting maintenance issues before a job fails.
